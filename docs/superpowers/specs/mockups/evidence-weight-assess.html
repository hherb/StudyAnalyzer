You answer the tool's questions (about 22 for RoB 2). The domain verdict is filled in from the official algorithm, and you can override it, with your override stored. Slower, but every disagreement can be traced to a question, and conformance is checked everywhere. Clicking a question can mark the sentence you based it on.
